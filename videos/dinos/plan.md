# dinos: "Los dinosaurios no eran como en las películas" (horizontal 16:9, ~6.5 min)

Idea: movie myths vs what we know (raptor size and feathers, the invented venom and frill, the T. rex posture, eyesight and roar, feathers and colours, caring mums, sauropods on land, who lived when, what isn't a dinosaur, the asteroid in Yucatán) and the promise: birds ARE dinosaurs — grandma's hen is one.

Style: mixed (3D world, Tico and Dota and the people drawn in 2D on paper cut-outs). Prefix z3/Z3. First horizontal video: the pipeline now takes "format": "16:9" (1920x1080).

## Cast
- One dinosaur rig (z3Dino) for every species: organic torso, neck and tail as tapered tubes that sway, a head with a jaw, blinking eyes, legs with knees that walk, little arms, and per-species extras (feathers, crests, the invented frill, horns and frill, plates and tail spikes, sails). Species: movie raptor, real raptor (turkey size, feathered), Deinonychus, T. rex, Yutyrannus, invented spitter, real Dilophosaurus, Sinosauropteryx (striped tail), Maiasaura and babies, Brontosaurus, Stegosaurus, Triceratops, Spinosaurus, Dimetrodon, crocodile, horse.
- Birds (hen, pigeon, turkey), pterosaurs, mosasaur, shark, raccoon.
- 2D: Tico, Dota (plush T. rex hood, cardboard frill, freeze, sweat, thermometer), a kid, a caveman.
- Pop-culture nods are kept generic (a rider whose horse becomes a raptor, a giant upright monster on a toy city, a caveman riding a long neck, a dark shadow peeking): genre tropes, never lookalikes of existing characters.

## Sets
Room, studio (dark backdrop, cream floor, coloured bokeh), prehistoric land (ferns, cycads, a smoking volcano; dust can darken it), paper (old drawings), park, sea, map table (world map, Mexico with Chicxulub).

## Camera, effects, sound
Presenter Tico (and Dota) drawn on top in the lower corners; slow trucks and dolly-ins, lens punches on keys, a hand-held drift, depth of field, dust in the light, lime ring bursts on key moments. Labels, counters, stamps (INVENTADO, SE CREE, SE DISCUTE) drawn inside the 3D frame. Music in C at 104 bpm; foley per scene (stomps, rumbles, splashes, clucks, coos, tape, the impact).
