# primos: ¿venimos del chango? (mixed style)

Idea: we don't come from the chimp: we share a grandpa (a species that no longer exists, ~7 million years ago in Africa); the proof is in the DNA (~1 in 100 letters differ) and in chromosome 2 (two chromosomes fused: two caps stuck in the middle); Darwin said it in 1871, a magazine joked otherwise; Toumaï may be the grandpa's face.

Style: mixed (3D things, characters drawn in 2D on paper cut-outs). Prefix q3/Q3. Visual references from the user's clips: the evolution stairs that break, you and your cousin with a crossed arrow, the grandpa in a round frame with family lines, two branches (blue to the chimp, red to you) with counters, the DNA helix and a card, strips and a 10x10 grid of letters with one red, rows of chromosomes (blue 24, red 23 with a gap), yellow caps like shoelace tips, a magnifier, Darwin with his 1871 book, the magazine that multiplies with a year counter, the skull in the sunset desert with its label.

## Cast (2D, original): tú (red hoodie), tu primo (blue striped sweater), el abuelito (bald, glasses, white moustache), the chimp, the gorilla, the ancestor (purple ape: neither chimp nor human), Darwin (bald, white beard, black suit, green 1871 book). Portrait mode draws only the head for the round frames.
## Sets: dark studio with a cream floor and a halo (most scenes; blue variant for the grid and the ending), the paper set (Darwin), the sunset desert (Toumaï), the banner.
## Sound: D, 100 bpm; curious → wonder → energetic (branches) → tense (chromosomes) → triumphant (fusion, Toumaï) → playful ending. Foley: crumble, ticks for counters, pops, clicks, a soft boom at the fusion, paper for the magazines, desert wind.
