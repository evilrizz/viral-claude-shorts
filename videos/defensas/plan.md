# defensas: how the body fights viruses and bacteria (mixed style)

Idea: your body wins small wars every day: barriers, alarm, eaters (macrophages), killers (T cells), antibodies, fever, and memory cells (why chickenpox rarely comes twice; vaccines teach the same photo).

Style: the mixed one (3D world, Tico and Dota drawn in 2D on paper cut-outs facing the camera, same acting brain as avion-mix). Prefix d3/D3.

## Cast
- Tico, Dota (2D): extra looks per frame via `fx`: chickenpox spots, hurt finger (red, swollen, throbbing), thermometer in the mouth, sneeze (inhale 0..0.75, achoo), sad face; `force(gesture)` adds itch, finger, sick, pinch, sneeze.
- Bacterium: green glossy capsule, darker spots, three whip tails, mean grin; sweats and gets scared.
- Virus: purple ball with knobbed spikes, sneaky half-closed look and wide grin; laughs. Chickenpox virus: same in red-pink.
- Macrophage: big soft white blob with feelers, kind eyes, a mouth that opens wide and a gulp.
- Body cell: see-through pink skin, nucleus, face; fills with virus copies, raises a red flag with a virus piece, bursts into pieces.
- T cell: blue blob with a green army helmet, determined brows, two fists that punch.
- B / memory cell: orange blob with a nozzle that fires antibodies (yellow Y with red pincers).
- Props: padlock with "?", photo album and germ photos, big thermometer, syringe with the chickenpox photo, antibiotic pill with angry eyes, prescription card, sheet of paper, bed.

## Sets
- Room: wooden floor, mint wall, window with sky, a picture, warm sun from the window, light shaft, dust.
- Micro (inside Dota's finger): deep red space, soft pink cells floating far (blurred by the depth of field), bumpy tissue floor, pink fog; hot = orange for the fever. Tico presents from the corner.
- Banner (end): rays, SUSCRÍBANSE over the Idio-two logo; Dota about to sneeze, Tico pinches his nose.

## Camera, effects, sound
Room two-shot (8.4 m, slight dolly at each cut and lens punches on keys); micro mid shots; a push into Dota's finger with a red flash into the micro world; rack focus via D3_FOCUS_OVR. Tags name each new defender once. Foley per scene (siren, chomps, burp, sneeze, soft boom, boings), music in F at 108 bpm following the curve curious → tense → energetic/epic → wonder → triumphant → playful.
